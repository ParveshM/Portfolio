## Platform

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Freelance clients**: business owners and agencies deciding whether to hire Muhammed to build a website or web app for them. Their job is to judge whether this person can take their product from idea to a live, working site.
- **Recruiters and hiring leads**: people screening him for a full-time React or full-stack role. Their job is to check real experience, shipped work and stack depth quickly, then reach out.

The site must work for both of them. It shouldn't lean toward either one.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Muhammed Parvesh M, a software developer working mainly in React and Node.js (MERN, Next.js, TypeScript). It exists to win freelance engagements and full-time roles. It succeeds when a visitor from either audience gets in touch through LinkedIn, GitHub or email.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every claim of capability should point at a shipped thing: a live site, a stack in production, a real role.
2. **Show the whole pipeline.** Wherever it fits, surface the backend, infrastructure and integration depth behind the frontend. That depth is the differentiator.
3. **Serve both readers fast.** A client and a recruiter should each find what they need (what he can build / what he has done) without wading through content meant for the other.
4. **Every path ends in contact.** Wherever a visitor finishes reading, the next step to reach him should be obvious.
5. **Honest attribution.** Describe team and employer work as contribution, and keep academic work labelled as academic.
